Levels

Your apps. Your volume.

Independent app volume for macOS 14.2 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel. No audio driver or developer tools needed.

Install once

  1. Drag Levels.app onto Applications in this disk image. Eject the disk image.
  2. Open Levels from Applications or Spotlight. Look for the sliders icon in your menu bar, or the island around your MacBook’s notch.
  3. Click Enable App Volume and allow System Audio Recording. Click Check Again if prompted. If macOS asks you to quit and reopen, do that first.
  4. Play audio in an app, then lower its slider. Other apps keep their own levels. Use the speaker button to mute.
Audio stays on your Mac. Levels processes audio in memory to change volume. It never saves or uploads it. No microphone or Accessibility access is required.

Make it yours

Open the mixer’s ••• options → Display Mode to switch between Dynamic Island and Menu Bar Popover. Your choice, app volumes and mute states are remembered. Active apps appear first.

The island expands when you click it or hover briefly beside the notch. The menu bar sliders icon also opens the mixer. Use Escape, an outside click or the close control to dismiss it.

If macOS blocks a test build

A file marked test has a local development signature and has not been notarized by Apple. After trying to open a copy you trust, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm. Follow Apple’s instructions; keep the Mac’s security protections enabled.

If the app sliders stay disabled

Use ••• options → Audio Privacy Settings. Enable Levels under Screen & System Audio Recording (the name varies by macOS version), reopen Levels if required, then click Check Again. Existing installations may still show its former name, Sound Mixer.

Some outputs have hardware volume controls; the system slider is hidden for those. If an app uses multiple outputs or a multichannel device, choose one mono or stereo output in that app, then retry.

Restore or remove

Reset All restores every app to 100% and unmutes it. Pause App Mixing or Quit Levels restores normal audio while keeping your saved levels. To uninstall, quit Levels and move it from Applications to Trash. There are no drivers or background helpers to remove.
